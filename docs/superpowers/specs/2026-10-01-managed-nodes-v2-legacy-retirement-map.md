# Managed nodes v2: legacy test retirement map

This map records why the former `hydra.services.nodes` product and its transport/state
contracts were removed, and where still-applicable invariants are exercised. The old
product used a separate `NodeConfig`/generation protocol and product contracts for
withdraw/detach, remote cookies and contract-version-gated node upgrades. Those are
not aliases or migrations for the v2 `NodeDefinition`/operation/receipt model.

## Deliberately retired product-only behavior

- `test_node_cookie_import.py` and cookie-menu cases depended on copying a separate
  old-format cookie document. No v2 protected cookie-import flow is exposed; a
  cookie-requiring transport must not be treated as supported merely because a field
  exists in the old wizard.
- Offline withdraw, detach-without-cleanup, old three-way removal, and their old
  `test_node_management_edits.py`/`test_node_manager.py`/`test_node_scenario.py` fixtures
  belonged to the rejected product contract. The v2 remover has its own explicit
  confirmed operation and recovery tests; no silent alias translates the old actions.
- The old role marker, node-only CLI entrypoints, `/v5` control contract, cookie endpoint,
  contract-version updater gate and mTLS service unit are removed. The base `upgrade.sh`
  remains transactional and no longer imports the retired node contract.
- Fake-runtime cascade tests remain useful for fail-closed state/security orchestration,
  but cannot establish a real two-hop route, client check, exported cascade profile, or
  billing behavior. Those production requirements remain open.

  seam to `run_sync_cycle` and keeps ordering plus partial-error assertions. The old

  tests with v2 form preflight, conditional-field cleanup, bounds and secret rejection.

  `ConfirmedProfile` contract instead of the removed NodeClientProfile DTO.

  `hydra-managed-node.service` is removed while the retired `hydra-node-control.service`
  is absent.

  and cutover-order assertions; its old control-contract version assertion is replaced by
  an assertion that no retired node contract gate remains.

  reintroduced, while preserving the independent subscription and sync entrypoint
  delegation tests.
